PriceWatchPro browser extension

Price history right on Walmart, Best Buy and Home Depot product pages, so you can see in a couple of seconds whether a sale is a real price drop.

Add to Chrome, free Visit PriceWatchPro

What it shows

  • The current price, plus the lowest and highest prices PriceWatchPro has recorded
  • A chart of every recorded price change, and an "At its lowest tracked price" flag
  • A toolbar popup with a short written analysis, such as how today's price compares with its 90-day average
  • One click to the full chart, where you can set a free price-drop email alert

Privacy

No analytics, no tracking, and no access to your browsing history. On a supported product page the extension sends only that product's public item number to PriceWatchPro to look up its price history, without cookies. Every network call is in one file, background.js, which you can read on GitHub. Full policy: pricewatchpro.com/privacy.

How the prices are collected

PriceWatchPro records each product's price from the stores' own data and keeps every change, so each history keeps growing. The details are on How we track prices.
